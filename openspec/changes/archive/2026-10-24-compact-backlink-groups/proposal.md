## Why

Backlink results repeat navigation text and display overlapping source outlines more than once. Readers need the source page, the relevant outline and a short route back to its original location.

## What Changes

- Present each source page as a compact card with linked ancestor breadcrumbs and labelled source controls.
- Display overlapping referring outlines once while retaining each authored context and its filters/counts.
- Show the safe relevant property rows and link to the original property disclosure.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `published-linked-references`: compact source groups, single outline copies and safe property snippets.

## Impact

Shared backlink rendering, property-view row reuse, reference-copy markup, default styles and nested-entry controller handling.
