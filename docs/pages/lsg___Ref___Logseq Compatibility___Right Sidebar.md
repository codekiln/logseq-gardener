- # Right Sidebar Compatibility
	- `lsg` publishes Logseq's right sidebar: the Toggle right sidebar button and `t r` open a column of cards at 40% of the window, Shift-click on a page link, tag, bullet, block reference or breadcrumb adds a page, block or Block references card at the top, each card collapses and closes and has Logseq's card menu, the top bar adds Contents, Page graph and Help cards, the column resizes, and the cards survive navigation and reloads. Cards show the published page files, fetched when a reader adds them, where Logseq renders cards from the whole graph it has already loaded. The Help card lists only the shortcuts the published site supports, the card menu works from the keyboard, below 640px there is no sidebar and Shift-click follows the link, and pages that exist only because something references them have no card.
	  id:: 4f926c78-f61a-40f8-ab04-1c11bf943ba7
	- Measured on 2026-10-26 against the `gh-pages` build of the encode garden (`gh-pages` commit `8132c95`, served locally, light theme, 1280×800 and 390×844) and an `lsg` build of the whole garden at commit `0ce74d6` from `lsg` `1e72969` (`mise run parity:build`). The OpenSpec change [match-logseq-right-sidebar](https://github.com/codekiln/logseq-gardener/tree/main/openspec/changes/archive/2026-10-27-match-logseq-right-sidebar) then built the sidebar, and [published-right-sidebar](https://github.com/codekiln/logseq-gardener/blob/main/openspec/specs/published-right-sidebar/spec.md) holds its requirements; [published-page-graph](https://github.com/codekiln/logseq-gardener/blob/main/openspec/specs/published-page-graph/spec.md) holds those of the Page graph card.
	- ## What Logseq's published site shows
		- Source links point at the local `logseq/og` checkout, `6e7afa8eb` (`frontend` = `src/main/frontend`). Measured live values come first and win where they differ from the checkout or from [[lsg/Ref/Logseq Published Site/Clickable Prototype]]. The Prototype board differs from the live build in several places, listed where they occur: it opens the left sidebar by default, gives the card menu a different item list, describes Help as a list of ten shortcuts, and says new cards open with a page link and the page's outline.

			- **Toggle button.** `button.toggle-right-sidebar` with title `Toggle right sidebar` and the 20px Tabler icon `layout-sidebar-right`, at the right end of the header after More ([`right_sidebar.cljs`](https://github.com/logseq/og/blob/6e7afa8eb/src/main/frontend/components/right_sidebar.cljs#L27-L34)). It is not rendered below 640px.
			- **`t r`** toggles the sidebar ([`shortcut/config.cljs`](https://github.com/logseq/og/blob/6e7afa8eb/src/main/frontend/modules/shortcut/config.cljs#L478-L479), [`handler/ui.cljs`](https://github.com/logseq/og/blob/6e7afa8eb/src/main/frontend/handler/ui.cljs#L38-L41)). `alt+shift+c` toggles the Contents card and opens the sidebar with it; pressing it again removes the card and closes the sidebar ([`handler/ui.cljs`](https://github.com/logseq/og/blob/6e7afa8eb/src/main/frontend/handler/ui.cljs#L61-L67)). `shift+/` is bound to toggle Help, but pressing it on the live build did nothing in the measured browser.
			- **Default state:** closed ([`state.cljs`](https://github.com/logseq/og/blob/6e7afa8eb/src/main/frontend/state.cljs#L87-L88)). The encode garden does not set `:default-home :sidebar`; when a garden sets it, Logseq adds a card for each listed page (or Contents) on start ([`container.cljs`](https://github.com/logseq/og/blob/6e7afa8eb/src/main/frontend/components/container.cljs#L691-L706)).
			- **Empty sidebar.** When the sidebar has no cards, it shows a Contents card ([`right_sidebar.cljs`](https://github.com/logseq/og/blob/6e7afa8eb/src/main/frontend/components/right_sidebar.cljs#L471-L476)). That card is a placeholder, not a stored card: the first Shift-click replaces it, and after Close all, `t r` shows it again.
			- **Closing.** The sidebar closes when its last card is closed, after Close all, or by `t r` and the toggle. Escape does not close it ([`state.cljs`](https://github.com/logseq/og/blob/6e7afa8eb/src/main/frontend/state.cljs#L1145-L1152)).
			- **Saved state.** `localStorage` key `ls-right-sidebar-state` holds the open state, the cards (as database ids) and their collapsed flags, for example `{:blocks (["local" 149550 :block-ref]), :collapsed {149550 false}, :open? true}`. Logseq writes it when the sidebar opens or closes or the number of cards changes, not when a card is only collapsed, and restores it after the graph loads, also when publishing ([`handler/ui.cljs`](https://github.com/logseq/og/blob/6e7afa8eb/src/main/frontend/handler/ui.cljs#L43-L59), [`theme.cljs`](https://github.com/logseq/og/blob/6e7afa8eb/src/main/frontend/components/theme.cljs#L67-L72)). Following a link keeps the cards; a reload restores them expanded.

			- Every card renders from the in-browser database, so the sidebar opens at once, but only after the whole graph has loaded: `static/js/db.gz` (19.8 MB) and `main.js` (7.5 MB). 	- ## What lsg publishes
		- From 640px wide, the header ends with a Toggle right sidebar button after More, with the `layout-sidebar-right` icon, an accessible name and `aria-expanded`. The button and `t r` open and close the sidebar; it starts closed on a first visit and closes when its last card is closed, after Close all, or by the toggle and `t r`. [[lsg/Ref/Site Navigation]] describes it for readers.
		- While open, the sidebar is a column of 40% of the window, at most 60vw unless the reader resizes it, and the header and main column use the remaining width: at 1280px on `tmux`, a 512px sidebar beside a 768px main column, with no sideways scroll. The card list has 16px side padding and 150px bottom padding and scrolls on its own.
		- Shift-click on a page reference, tag, page-valued property, Pages tagged with row, reference group title, page embed header, query result, All pages row or plugin-derived page link adds a card for that page. Shift-click on a bullet adds a card for that block; on a block reference, a Block references card for the referenced block. In Linked References and query results, a bullet adds a card for its block on the source page and a breadcrumb crumb adds a Block references card for the crumb's block, as in Logseq.
		- The main page and its URL stay the same. A new card goes to the top, expanded; a target already in the sidebar moves to the top and expands. Links and bullets inside cards behave the same way, and a plain click on a link inside a card opens the page in the main column.
		- A page card shows the page as the main column shows it, without its title and without Unlinked References: properties, outline, Pages tagged with, Linked References closed until opened, and Hierarchy. A block card shows the block and its children under a header holding the block's breadcrumb. A Block references card shows `Block references`, a breadcrumb row and the block with its children.
		- Card content comes from the published page file, fetched when the card is added or expanded, and reference bodies come from the page's `_refs/` fragment files when a reader opens the section, as on the page itself. A card whose page is no longer published says `This page is not published.` and offers Close.
		- Each card header is a button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` holding a chevron, an icon (the page's `icon::` when it has one) and the title truncated with an ellipsis. A Close card button and a Card menu button follow, each with an accessible name. A middle click on the header closes the card, and a collapsed card shows only its header.
		- The card menu offers Close; Close others and Close all with two or more cards; Collapse or Expand; Collapse others, Collapse all and Expand all with two or more cards; and Open as page for page cards and Slides cards, in Logseq's groups. It is a `role="menu"` that opens from the keyboard with focus on its first item, moves with arrow keys, and closes on Escape, on a click outside it and after an item is chosen, returning focus to the Card menu button.
		- The top bar holds Contents, Page graph and Help buttons, each adding its card at the top. An empty sidebar shows a Contents card with the outline of the published `contents` page without its title; on the encode garden that is one empty bullet from `pages/contents.md`, which `lsg` publishes like any selected page. `alt+shift+c` adds the Contents card and opens the sidebar, or removes it and closes the sidebar when it is the only card. The Page graph card draws the current page's neighbourhood, as [[lsg/Ref/Logseq Compatibility/Graph View]] describes.
		- The Help card lists the shortcuts and gestures the published site supports under `Keyboard shortcuts`: `t l`, `t r`, `t t`, `t w`, `g h`, `g a`, `g g` when the graph view is published, `g j` when a journals feed exists, `mod k` when search is published, `alt shift c` and Shift-click. It links to no site outside the garden.
		- The column's left edge is a focusable separator with an accessible name. Dragging it, or Left and Right in 5px steps, resizes the column between the larger of 320px and 10% of the window and 70% of the window.
		- The open state, width, cards in order and each card's collapsed flag are saved under `logseq-gardener-right-sidebar` in browser localStorage, by route and block identifier, and restored on every page. A saved open sidebar reserves the column's width before first paint, and card content loads after the page's `load` event, so the title shows first. With storage unavailable, the state lasts for the current page.
		- When there is no saved state, `:default-home {:sidebar …}` in `logseq/config.edn` supplies the starting cards: each name that resolves to a published page or alias adds a page card, `"Contents"` adds the Contents card, and any other name produces a publish diagnostic.
		- A block card addresses its block by the `id::` UUID when the block has one, and otherwise by the position identifier block zoom uses, such as `3.1.2`.
		- A host that serves pages at rewritten addresses keeps Shift-click working, because the sidebar recognizes page links inside `.page-reference` or with the class `page-ref` or `tag`, block references by `block-ref-link`, and links whose address holds `#source-block-`. Each card stores its route relative to the folder that serves `lsg-sidebar.js`, so a saved card opens the same page from every route. [[lsg/Ref/Host Integration API]] describes this for hosts.
		- Below 640px, the toggle is absent, `t r` and `alt+shift+c` do nothing, and Shift-click follows the link as the browser does.
		- The SDK answers what a card shows: page routes for titles, aliases and tags from the name index, block identifiers from the outline, breadcrumbs from the reference index, and `:default-home :sidebar` from the bounded EDN reader. The publisher renders the toggle, the styles in `lsg-sidebar.css` and the behaviour in `lsg-sidebar.js`, which loads on a reader's first use, or after the page's `load` event when the sidebar was left open. `lsg-shell.js` holds only the hook that loads it.
	- ## Differences from Logseq
		- Cards fetch the published page files when a reader adds them. Logseq renders every card from the whole graph, which it loads with every page; `lsg` loads no graph in the browser.
		- The Help card lists the published site's shortcuts and gestures and links nowhere outside the garden. Logseq's Help card links to Logseq's forum, documentation and bug tracker and lists its 157 shortcuts, most of them for editing.
		- The card menu is a keyboard menu with focusable items, arrow keys and Escape. Logseq's menu items are not focusable.
		- Below 640px, `t r` does nothing and Shift-click follows the link. Logseq ignores the Shift-click there and opens a 156px column on `t r`, which is too narrow to read.
		- The saved state records cards by route and block identifier and includes collapsed flags and width, written on every change. Logseq stores database ids, which a static site does not have, and does not save a collapse on its own.
		- Pages that exist only because something references them, such as `VSCode/Terminal`, are not published, so `lsg` has no card for them. Logseq opens a card for such a page.
		- Cards cannot be edited or dragged to reorder them, blocks cannot be dragged into or out of the sidebar, and there is no History card.
		- Search results do not open in the sidebar: Shift+Enter, Shift-click on a result and search cards (Cmd+Enter) are Logseq features `lsg` has no equivalent for.
		- Logseq's dark-theme values were not measured; the sidebar uses `lsg`'s theme tokens.
	- ## Performance
		- The sidebar adds only the toggle button to a page's first paint and the hook to `lsg-shell.js`. Each card fetches one published page file, so a typical card transfers a page of about 27 KB, about 8 KB gzip, and the browser can reuse it later. Fetched page HTML is cached by route for the life of the tab, at most 20 routes, and a collapsed card drops its body and renders it again from the cache when expanded.
		- The `[sidebar]` table of [`scripts/parity/budgets.toml`](https://github.com/codekiln/logseq-gardener/blob/main/scripts/parity/budgets.toml) holds the budgets: `lsg-sidebar.js` at most 6.5 KB gzip, Shift-click to a card's outline at p95 within 300 ms on desktop and 1,500 ms on the phone profile, one card's transfer at most 60 KB gzip, and a peak phone heap of at most 20 MB after ten cards. Every fixture page's first paint stays within the `[desktop]` and `[phone]` budgets, and the `[shell]` `script_kb` budget covers the hook. `parity:budgets` checks the sizes in CI.
		- On 2026-10-27, with the change on garden commit `cc1bb2e`, Shift-click to a card's outline took 27 ms at p95 over ten page cards on desktop, the `Person` card transferred 14.2 KB gzip, and the heap after ten cards was 3.1 MB.
		- [[lsg/Ref/Logseq Compatibility/Performance]] records the run of 2026-10-28 on `tmux`: 13 cards, 58 ms at p95 from Shift-click to card, 2.2 MB of heap after the cards, `lsg-sidebar.js` 6.2 KB gzip and the largest card 13.9 KB gzip. Two published pages exceed the 60 KB card budget as cards and as pages: `Person/Gergely Orosz/Pod/26/09 Design Engineering with Maggie Appleton` (88.1 KB gzip) and `Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference` (62.3 KB gzip). Their content, not the shell, makes up the size, so `lsg` writes no content-only page fragments.
	- ## Before the change
		- The `lsg` build measured on 2026-10-26, from `1e72969`, had no right sidebar. These facts come from `crates/logseq-gardener-sdk/src/site/shell.rs`, `shell.js`, `outline.js` and the [published-app-shell](https://github.com/codekiln/logseq-gardener/blob/main/openspec/specs/published-app-shell/spec.md) specification, checked against the whole-garden build.
		- **No right sidebar.** The header held Toggle left sidebar, Home, Search garden and More. The app shell specification required that the header contain only controls whose destination exists, and named the right sidebar as one that did not yet.
		- **`t r`** was not handled; the shell handled `t l`, `t t`, `t w`, `g h`, `g a` and `g j`.
		- **Shift-click on a link** was left to the browser, so Chromium opened the page in a new window (measured on `tmux`).
		- **Shift-click on a bullet** zoomed into the block like a plain click (`tmux` → `#zoom/1.1`); `outline.js` did not check the Shift key.
		- **Block anchors.** Only blocks with an `id::` property got `id="block-<uuid>"`; other blocks were reached by position (`#zoom/1.1`) or by `source-block-N` spans.
		- **Unresolved references** (pages with no file, such as `VSCode/Terminal`) rendered as plain text, so there was nothing to Shift-click.
		- **Contents.** The build had no `contents` page; the comparison harness listed it among Logseq-only pages. The parity build selected the root `contents` while the page's title is `Contents`; the harness fix published it.
		- **Reference fragments.** Linked and unlinked reference bodies were already separate files under `_refs/`, loaded when a section opens (`lsg-references.js`).
		- **Sizes.** `lsg-shell.js` was 5,662 bytes of the 6 KB `[shell]` budget. On `tmux` the header and left sidebar markup was 3,522 bytes. Pages were small: median 27 KB, of which about 23.6 KB (6.7 KB gzipped) was the inlined head and shell before `<main>`; the largest, `Person`, was 508 KB (52 KB gzipped).
