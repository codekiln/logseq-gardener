- # Diátaxis
	- [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/) sorts documentation by what the reader needs. Each page of the `lsg` documentation serves one need, and its title and its `tags::` property name its kind, following codekiln's convention [Logseq/Entity/Diataxis](https://github.com/codekiln/logseq-encode-garden/blob/main/pages/Logseq___Entity___Diataxis.md).
	- | Kind | Serves a reader who wants to | Title | Tag |
	  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
	  | [[Diataxis/How To]] | do a task | `lsg/How To/<task>` | `[[Diataxis/How To]]` |
	  | [[Diataxis/Tutorial]] | learn by doing | `lsg/Tutorial/<goal>` | `[[Diataxis/Tutorial]]` |
	  | [[Diataxis/Reference]] | look something up | `lsg/Ref/<subject>` | `[[Diataxis/Reference]]` |
	  | [[Diataxis/Explanation]] | understand why | `lsg/<concept>`, or `lsg` for the topic itself | `[[Diataxis/Explanation]]` |
	- Each kind page lists the pages of its kind in its Pages tagged with section.
