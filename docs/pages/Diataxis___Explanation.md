- # Explanation
	- An explanation page says why `lsg` works as it does: the ideas behind it, its design and its trade-offs. In [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/explanation/), explanation serves understanding.
	- An explanation page here is titled `lsg/<concept>`, or `lsg` for the topic itself, and carries `tags:: [[Diataxis/Explanation]]`. Codekiln's convention also calls this kind Concept, so `[[Diataxis/Concept]]` opens this page. The page shape follows [Logseq/Entity/Diataxis/Explanation](https://github.com/codekiln/logseq-encode-garden/blob/main/pages/Logseq___Entity___Diataxis___Explanation.md) and [Logseq/Entity/Concept](https://github.com/codekiln/logseq-encode-garden/blob/main/pages/Logseq___Entity___Concept.md): an overview, the context, the key principles, how it works and examples.
	- The Pages tagged with section below lists every explanation page. [[Diataxis]] lists the other kinds.
